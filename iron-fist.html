Summit Policy

Iron Fist

1

Introduction

Crime in New Zealand is a big issue, with a 14% increase in minor theft offences. Laws around these crimes are often strict with very little to no punishments, this encourages the frequency of minor offences as people believe there are no consequences for doing these.

2

Problem

There are about 214 criminal proceedings per 10 thousand people. This means thousands of people are let off without punishment. A change in policies on Law and Order can heavily crack down on the level of small crimes, helping all of New Zealand stay safe and secure.

3

Current Policies

The Act Party currently has a policy of Three Strikes for Burglary, while this has helped reduce the amount of bigger crimes, it doesn’t affect the lower level crimes as much.

4

Our Policy

Summit’s policy, Iron Fist, will introduce a wide range of fines and punishments depending on the level of crime. Iron Fist will keep the current policies on major offending and focus on controlling the less threatening ones. The main way to do this will be by issuing large fines for minor offences, especially theft. The money received from these fines will go towards lowering government debt explained in policy one. Society will quickly start seeing a reduction in these areas of crime because of the large fines issued to minor offenders, it quickly becomes not with the risk of punishment.

5

Summary

Iron Fist will toughen the consequences on minor crimes as well as assisting the journey in reducing government debt and inflation.
