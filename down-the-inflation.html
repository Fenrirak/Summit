Down the Inflation

1

Introduction

Government debt is a major issue in New Zealand. The more debt a government is in, the more inflation is caused.This is because when the government uses loaned money, it sends a surge of inexistant money into the economy. When there is more money in an economy with the exact same amount of resources, it causes inflation for everything for everyone. Due to inflation, many families can not afford the high prices, causing them to take out personal loans or increase credit card debt. The average family in New Zealand is in $220,000 to $260,000 of debt. The cycle then starts again. The government debt causes inflation, inflation causes personal debt, personal debt sends another surge of inexistant money causing inflation again. This continuing cycle of debt and inflation is forever problematic affecting everyone who lives in that society.

2

Problem

The 2026 Budget says that New Zealand’s government debt is more than twice as high as it was 7 years ago (During Covid!). With this increased debt, the government is paying about $9 billion in interest. That’s $9 billion less that can be used for the development of infrastructure, public services, and more! A change in policy will control unnecessary government spending and improve the economy.

3

Current Policies

The Act party currently has a policy for a smaller more affordable government. This could help reduce spending due to less wages to pay, but it can also cause increased workloads for the remaining staff or in extreme cases slow down and reduce the efficiency of the government.

4

Our Policy

Summit Party’s policy on this economic matter will reduce unnecessary government spending and control its expenses. This policy will let the government be more mindful where their money is being spent, and where it could be reduced. With this policy, Down the Inflation, it will benefit the New Zealand society through lowering the rate of inflation and reduce the surges of inexistant money causing it. A downside of this policy will be a small rise in costs of public services as the government would require redirecting parts of the subsidy in order to reduce the government debt. This download is unfortunately inevitable with Down the Inflation, but Summit will do its best to keep the price change to a minimum.

5

Summary

Down the Inflation will briefly raise costs of some public services, but in the long term, lower the government debt and ultimately slow the rate of inflation.
